Privacy Policy

Your local grill journal

Ember helps you plan grill nights, record food observations and create personal recap videos. Grill locations entered as text, charcoal notes, food records, fire-zone events, photos, video clips, audio recordings and exported reels are stored in the application container on your device. Ember does not require an account and does not operate a community or messaging service.

Camera, photo library and microphone

We ask for permission when you choose a feature that needs it. Camera access allows you to take grill photos or record clips. Photo library access allows you to select media and save completed reels. Microphone access allows you to record voice notes and narration. You may decline permission and continue using the remaining journal features. You can change permissions in device Settings.

Speech recognition

If you choose voice input and allow speech recognition, your recorded voice note is processed through Apple speech recognition services to produce text. Processing may use Apple servers depending on device capabilities and availability. Apple receives the audio needed for that request under its own privacy practices. You can type notes instead. Voiceover recording does not require speech transcription.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. Ember receives transaction identifiers, product information and verification results to deliver purchased Reels and prevent duplicate delivery. We do not receive your payment card details. Your Reel balance and delivery history are stored on this device. Clearing local data erases the remaining balance; transaction deduplication records are retained to prevent duplicate grants.

Notifications

Ember requests permission for notifications when you first reach the main console. You may decline or change that choice in device Settings. Ember does not use notifications to collect grill media.

Third-party services and sharing

Ember does not send your photo, video, audio or grill journal to third-party content-generation services. Apple provides payment, photo-library and optional speech services. Media leaves the app only through the system features you choose, such as saving to Photos, sharing an exported reel, or permitted speech processing. Your device backup settings may include application data in an Apple device backup. When you view a remote legal page, the hosting provider may receive ordinary connection information such as an IP address.

Retention and deletion

You can delete individual grill nights and their associated app copies, or use Clear All Data to erase the local library and remaining Reels. Files already saved to Photos or shared elsewhere are controlled separately and are not erased by clearing Ember. Removing the app may remove its local data. There is no app-managed cross-device recovery.

Security and choices

Ember relies on the iOS application sandbox and device protections. Keep your device protected and maintain any backups you need. No method of storage can guarantee against loss. We do not sell your personal journal or use advertising identifiers.

Contact and changes

Privacy questions can be addressed to ember.grill.journal@gmail.com. This policy may change when the app changes. Effective date: November 3, 2026.
